BITCOIN AS THE COMPANY RESERVE

A bitcoin treasury holds surplus in bitcoin on the company balance sheet. It is a capital preservation and savings policy: public and accountable.
Urban Environmental Ltd. entered the bitcon standard era in 2025. We understand that bitcoin enhances our goals and can help others do the same.

WHAT IS A TREASURY

A bitcoin treasury is an accumulation of wealth, we like to think of it as a reserve of energy for the future. Surplus reserves are held in bitcoin. Holdings are public, provable in real time, and treated like any other energy system we engineer.

WHY

The treasury enhances the long term strength of the company preserving energy so it can be allocated with careful consideration. When Urban Environmental is involved in large community projects, warranties and reliable ongoing product support are required. In this way the treasury provides verified measure of our commitment to the future.

GOOD ENGINEERING

Engineers like measurables and are challenged to create designs that tolerate environmental changes. The traditional financial system is changing and opaque where Bitcoin has stable and transparent rules. The treasury provides an accurate metric to guide better company actions.
